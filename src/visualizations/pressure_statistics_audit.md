## 1. Executive Summary

A numerical discrepancy was flagged between the Step 4 Pressure Index Validation Report and the Step 7 Cricket Analytics Visualization Report:

| Metric | Step 4 Report (`src/pressure_index_validation_report.md`) | Step 7 Report (`outputs/visualizations/cricket_analysis_report.md`) | Discrepancy ($\Delta$) |

2. **The Visualization Scripts Were Already Computing the Correct Values:** The visualization script [`src/visualizations/plot_pressure_analysis.py`](file:///c:/Users/ASUS/Desktop/supeRR_selector/src/visualizations/plot_pressure_analysis.py) dynamically computed `clean_df['Batting_PI'].mean()` ($35.69$) and `clean_df['Bowling_PI'].mean()` ($40.97$). The generated high-resolution chart images [`01_batting_pressure_distribution.png`](file:///c:/Users/ASUS/Desktop/supeRR_selector/outputs/visualizations/01_batting_pressure_distribution.png) and [`02_bowling_pressure_distribution.png`](file:///c:/Users/ASUS/Desktop/supeRR_selector/outputs/visualizations/02_bowling_pressure_distribution.png) plotted vertical summary lines at **$35.69$** and **$40.97$**.
3. **Root Cause Identified:** The numbers $41.77$ and $47.98$ existed **solely as a narrative text error in the written markdown report** `cricket_analysis_report.md` (and summarized in `src/visualizations/README.md`). During report drafting, unaligned placeholder draft figures were entered in Section Part A instead of transcribing the live outputs generated by `plot_pressure_analysis.py`.
4. **Resolution Completed:**

## 2. Detailed Investigation of the 5 Verification Points

### Point 1: Which Columns Are Used in Each Calculation

We audited column usage across the pipeline:

- **In Step 4 (`src/calculate_pressure_index.py`):**
  - The script calculates 12 phase-and-innings specific formula columns (`PI_B1_PP`, `PI_B1_MO`, `PI_B1_DO`, `PI_B2_PP`, `PI_B2_MO`, `PI_B2_DO` for batting; `PI_BO1_PP`, `PI_BO1_MO`, `PI_BO1_DO`, `PI_BO2_PP`, `PI_BO2_MO`, `PI_BO2_DO` for bowling).
  - It creates unified columns by coalescing the 6 mutually exclusive formula values:
    $$\text{Batting\_PI} = \text{coalesce}(\text{PI\_B1\_PP}, \dots, \text{PI\_B2\_DO})$$
    $$\text{Bowling\_PI} = \text{coalesce}(\text{PI\_BO1\_PP}, \dots, \text{PI\_BO2\_DO})$$
  - The validation report `src/pressure_index_validation_report.md` (Section 6.2) reported summary statistics calculated directly on `df['Batting_PI']` and `df['Bowling_PI']`.
- **In Step 7 (`src/visualizations/plot_pressure_analysis.py`):**
  - The script reads `data/processed/delivery_pressure_index.csv` and accesses `clean_df['Batting_PI']` and `clean_df['Bowling_PI']`.
  - Exactly the same columns were used in both steps. No secondary or intermediate column (such as team pressure $T$, individual pressure $I$, or single-phase columns) was substituted in the code.
- **Conclusion:** Both steps use the exact same unified metric columns `Batting_PI` and `Bowling_PI`. The discrepancy did not arise from column confusion.

### Point 3: Handling of Invalid and Flagged Deliveries

We audited how flagged deliveries affect pressure calculations:

1. **Target-Reached Tail Deliveries ($N = 233$):**
   - In these rows, $RRR$ becomes $0.0$, driving team pressure down.
   - Removing them raises clean population means by $+0.0095$ (Batting) and $+0.0178$ (Bowling).
2. **Target-Understated Matches ($38$ matches, $4,177$ clean deliveries):**
   - In Step 4.2 audit, 38 matches were identified where missing multi-run byes/leg-byes caused the reconstructed first-innings total to be 1 to 8 runs lower than official scores.
   - In Step 6.2 and Step 7, these deliveries were retained in the primary dataset and evaluated via sensitivity analysis.
   - Population excluding both tail deliveries and all 38 flagged matches ($N = 179,210$):
     - **Batting PI Mean:** $35.9139$ ($\approx 35.91$)
     - **Bowling PI Mean:** $41.0112$ ($\approx 41.01$)
     - Difference from clean baseline: $+0.22$ points for Batting, $+0.04$ points for Bowling.
- **Conclusion:** Whether flagged deliveries are included or excluded, mean Batting PI remains strictly in $[35.67, 35.92]$ and mean Bowling PI remains strictly in $[40.95, 41.02]$. Handling of flagged deliveries did not cause the $41.77$ / $47.98$ figures.
